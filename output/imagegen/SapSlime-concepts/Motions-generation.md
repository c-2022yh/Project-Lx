# Sap slime motion generation

Built-in image_gen. The approved SapSlime_v1 design and existing Slime idle/death frames were used as references. Sheet split and normalized to 640x640, center pivot, PPU 240; matching original neutral width and baseline. Existing animation order/times preserved in separate Enemy_SapSlime prefab. Single sprite PNGs under Assets/Sprites/Enemies/SapSlime.

## Generation prompt

Create a ready-to-slice 2D game sprite animation sheet, EXACTLY 3 COLUMNS and 2 ROWS of SIX equal SQUARE cells on a truly transparent alpha background. Landscape canvas, uniform regular cell spacing, generous transparent margins of at least 12% on each side within every cell. NO text, labels, grid lines, no extra frames. Image 1 is the LOCKED NEW CHARACTER DESIGN: low squat muted teal sap slime with thick dark teal outline, little drooping back-left nub, TWO SMALL solid CREAM eyes on right third with NO eye rings, ONE SMALL OLIVE LEAF embedded inside left half, broad pale sage highlight and dark teal lower band, simple soft cel shading, smooth rounded grounded blob with NO LEGS OR FEET. Keep it exactly recognizable, no external leaf on stalk. Images 2-4 are OLD animation motion references ONLY, NOT color/design references. All frames face RIGHT. Identical body scale, baseline, horizontal center and cell canvas across frames; the body remains planted, doesn't walk or jump. Top row is idle frames: cell1 neutral exactly image1; cell2 gently compressed 6-8% shorter and 3-4% wider with embedded leaf lagging a little; cell3 gently stretched 6-8% taller and 3-4% narrower with leaf following. All three idle frames same ground-contact line, same eyes and leaf, restrained breathing motion not exaggerated bouncing. Bottom row is death frames matching old animation progression: cell1 body squashed lower and broader, tiny cream eyes squeeze into closed cream angled strokes, embedded leaf tilts; cell2 body loses eyes and bursts into a low broad teal gel mound plus 2-3 LARGE detached teal gel lobes, small olive leaf released above mound; cell3 the body is gone, only 3 SMALL scattered teal droplets near the ground and that single olive leaf descending above them. No white sparkling particles, no ghost, no gore, no foreign colors. Death fragments stay wholly within own cell. For bottom row retain original idle scale and fixed ground line, do not enlarge small droplets to fill cell. The final death frame must be mostly transparent. No ground shadows or scenery. Production-friendly clean edges and alpha transparency.

## Alpha cleanup

Background extraction requested: preserve all six shapes and poses; remove exterior glow, halos, shadows and background; retain crisp outlines and fully transparent exterior. The returned image contains nonzero RGB in transparent areas; alpha compositing was checked on a light background.
